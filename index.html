Why these addons are small

Editor-only

No autoloads, no project settings, no nodes injected into your scenes. Disable the plugin and your project is byte-for-byte what it was.

No lock-in

Every edit is written into the resource Godot already uses. Curves behave like hand-authored ones; there is no side-car file to migrate and nothing to clean up.

Small on purpose

GDScript, no binaries, no third-party libraries. A pack you can read through in ten minutes and still understand in a year.

What Curve2D Editor does

  • Drag points — the curve and the 2D viewport update live.
  • Tangent handles — Smooth, Mirror In/Out and Break Tangents; smoothness is derived from the curve data, so it survives reopening, undo and scene reloads.
  • Add and remove points — double-click empty space to insert, Delete or Backspace to remove the selected one.
  • Real undo — one step per gesture, routed through the editor's own undo manager, so Ctrl+Z and the Undo History panel both work.
  • Zoom, pan, fit — wheel zooms about the cursor, middle-drag pans, F frames the curve. Display only; your point coordinates never change.

Requires Godot 4.3+ (developed and tested on 4.7.2-stable). Editor-side GDScript only — one download works on Windows, macOS and Linux.
